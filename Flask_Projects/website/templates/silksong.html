Hollow Knight: Silksong or more commonly called Silksong is a 2d action metroidvania sequel to the game Hollow Knight where the main character is brought into an unknown location and you search the region beating enemies and bosses as you go through, searching for the reason of your appearance while also improving your abilities, your skills, and also your management on health and your skill bar.

Although it may seem easy, it is difficult as it is a sequel to to game Hollow Knight, therefore it is expected that you've learned its movement from it. Furthermore, you'll encounter enemies that deal double the health, thus making it extremely challenging to get past multiple sections.
Its gimmick is very interesting because there are many different main skill types you're able to equip, giving you different base attacks, different equipment you're able to use and more, giving more versatility while also making suited combos for each set of equipment used.
