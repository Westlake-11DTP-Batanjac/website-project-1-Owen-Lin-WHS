Geometry dash is a diverse game to say the least. Although its base levels contain rather broad range of levels ranging in manageable difficulty, its creativity from players is what causes them to excel in their own ways. Geometry dash offers multitudes of level created from the play base alone with its create tab used to make 2d games ranging from a simple jumping game to a brilliant work of art and 3d or even an extremely difficult level.

Editor

The editor feature allows the user to make user generated content for others to play while also allowing them to create virtually anything ranging from plants verses zombies to rendering 3d objects on a 2d plane, making it versatile to everything and everyone.

It also contains a competitive fan base where people try and beat some of the most difficult levels classified as Extreme Demons in the difficulty charts of Geometry dash. It presents some of the most insane tricks possibly done such as frame perfects, patterns, and unique gimmicks in a short time, showing its player's dedication on them.
