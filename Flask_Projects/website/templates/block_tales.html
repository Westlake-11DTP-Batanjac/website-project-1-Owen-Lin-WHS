Block tales is an open world JRPG turn based story game from Roblox where you were brought back in time with your rival and you venture into different parts of Roblox to gather all the artifacts to rival your opponent. There are multitudes of mechanics including the leveling system and its skill based game loop such as build points, variety of ways to attack, and multiplayer co-operation. Build points or more commonly called BP is a point system that allows users to change what effects they can use such as gaining Skill points (SP) for every successful attack all the way up to extra defense to non-piercing attacks. There are also many utilities, skills, items, and supports you can use to aid you on your journey to help defeat your rival and it is you who makes your path to victory. Multiplayer is also supported in this game while power scaling is involved with more than 2 people but, it gives more diverse choices and action everyone can do to achieve their victory against enemies.
